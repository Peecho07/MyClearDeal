What does the $999 cover?+

Our help with one vehicle purchase: the free consultation, vehicle sourcing and price negotiation, fees and add-ons, your trade-in, your financing rate, protection products, a written recap and a final paperwork check.

Are there any other fees from you?+

No. There are no tiers, upgrades or add-ons, and we never take a percentage of your savings. Taxes, registration and the price of the car itself are paid to the dealer as usual, and we make sure each of those numbers is correct.

Do dealers pay you anything?+

No. You pay us, so we work for you. That keeps our advice focused on your best deal.

Does starting my deal charge me?+

No. Submitting the Start My Deal form only books your free consultation. We confirm the $999 fee with you on that call, before any work begins.

Is the Free Check My Deal really free?+

Yes. Send us your dealer quote and a member of our team will look it over and follow up with what looks fair and what to question. No obligation to hire us.
