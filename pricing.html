Where our fee earns its keep.

The sticker price is only one number. These are the places where buyers most often overpay without noticing.

Fees and add-ons

Extras you did not ask for

Market adjustments, pre-installed packages and padded fees can add up fast. We itemize every line and challenge what does not belong.

Market price

A price with nothing to compare it to

Most buyers don't know what the car actually sells for in their area. We check the number against real market data before you agree.

Trade-in

A trade valued low

A big discount on the new car means little if it was funded by a low trade-in offer. We keep the trade separate and backed by market value.
