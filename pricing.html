Pricing questions

Straight answers about our fee.

See all FAQs
What does the $995 cover?

Our help with one vehicle purchase: the free consultation, vehicle sourcing, negotiating the vehicle price, dealer fees and add-ons, and your trade-in, a market-data check on the price, and a written out-the-door recap before you agree.

Are there any other fees from you?

No. There are no tiers, upgrades or add-ons, and we never take a percentage of your savings. Taxes, registration and the price of the car itself are paid to the dealer as usual, and we make sure each of those numbers is correct.

Do dealers pay you anything?

No. You pay us, so we work for you. That keeps our advice focused on your best deal.

Does booking a free call charge me?

No. The form only books your free consultation. No payment is ever taken on this website. We confirm the $995 fee with you on that call, before any work begins.

Is my trade-in included?

Yes. Valuing your trade and keeping it separate from the new deal is part of the $995. No extra charge.

Is it worth $995 for my deal?

Every deal is different, so we never promise a dollar amount. On your free call we will tell you honestly whether we think we can help. If your deal already looks clean, we will say so.

Is the Free Check My Deal really free?

Yes. Send us your dealer quote and a member of our team will look it over and follow up with what looks fair and what to question. No obligation to hire us.
