Privacy

Privacy policy.

What we collect, why, and what we never do with it. Last updated November 8, 2026.

What we collect

Only what you type into our forms: your name, phone number and email, details about the car you want or the quote you have (vehicle, dealership, prices, fees, trade-in), your timeline and anything you add in the notes. We don't use tracking cookies, ads or analytics on this site.

How we use it

To review your quote, set up your free call, and work your deal if you hire us. We contact you by the method you choose: text, call or email.

Who sees it

Our team, and the services that run the site: your form is delivered to our inbox by FormSubmit, the site is hosted on GitHub Pages, and its fonts load from Google Fonts. These services can see basic technical details like your IP address.

We never sell your information, and we never share your name or contact details with a dealer without your OK.

Your choices

To see, correct or delete what you have sent us, email organizer@mycleardeal.com and we will take care of it.
