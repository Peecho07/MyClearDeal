For most people, a car is one of the biggest purchases they make, and one of the most stressful. You research the sticker price, then spend hours at the dealership while the real deal takes shape around you: a trade-in number, a monthly payment, a stack of products in the finance office and fees you did not expect.

That confusion is not your fault. The dealership works through this process every day. Most buyers go through it once every few years, often under time pressure and without anyone in their corner.

myClearDeal exists to close that gap. With over 25 years of automotive experience, we know where a deal has room, which fees are real and what a fair finance office looks like. We put that knowledge to work for you and explain every number in plain English, so you can decide with confidence instead of guesswork.

We keep our own pricing as clear as the deals we deliver: one $999 flat fee for one car. We do not take a cut of your savings and we are not paid by the dealer. Our only job is to help you make an informed decision you still feel good about long after you drive away.
