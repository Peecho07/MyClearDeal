Hero Program questions.

More answers about how we work are in our FAQ.

Is the Hero Program really free?

Yes. You get the full Done For You service, the same one others pay $995 for, at no cost. You still pay the dealer for the car, taxes and registration as usual, and we check every line.

Why do you do this?

Firefighters, officers, medical workers, teachers and service members look after everyone else. Getting a fair deal on a car is one way we can look after them.

What proof do I need?

One of these on your free call: a work ID or badge, a current license, a recent pay stub, or a military ID, VA card or DD-214. We only use it to confirm you qualify and never share it with a dealer.

I am retired or a veteran. Do I qualify?

Yes. Retired firefighters, officers, medical workers and teachers qualify, and so do veterans of every branch.

Can I use it for a family member?

It covers one car bought or leased for your own household. If a family member also serves, they can claim their own Hero deal.
