How we define a fair price

Fair isn't a feeling.

Before we agree to a number, we check it against what the same car is actually selling for in your area, what the dealer likely paid for it, and current incentives and rebates. If the price doesn't hold up, we keep working. You see the comparison in writing.

  • Live listings for the same model, trim and miles near you
  • Published invoice and dealer-cost data
  • The manufacturer's current incentives and rebates

Our scope

Price, fees and trade-in.

Financing, interest rates, or optional products like extended warranties or GAP. Those are between you and the dealer. They are always optional, and you can say no to any of them.
