# MyClearDeal content pillars (overrides the generic 40/30/20/10 split)

| Pillar | Share | What it covers | Formulas | Primary goal |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Buyer education** | 40% | how dealer quotes work, out-the-door price, doc fees and add-ons, trade-in kept separate, market checks, what to ask before you visit, the calculator | IG5 listicle, IG7 myth-buster, IG8 framework, IG10 how-I reel, IG1 number-first | saves, shares |
| **Proof and stories** | 25% | real deals we worked (with the client's OK), before/after quotes, the written recap, behind the scenes of a negotiation, the owner's own dealership background | IG6 before/after, IG4 story, IG1 number-first | follows, comments |
| **Heroes** | 20% | the Hero Program: who qualifies, proof, how to claim, thank-you posts on dates that matter to each field (Nurses Week, Teacher Appreciation Week, Veterans Day, First Responders Day) | IG5, IG9 pattern-interrupt reel, IG2 send-bait | shares (sends) |
| **Offer** | 15% | Done For You at $995, the free quote review, booking a free call | IG1, IG6, single clear next step | follows, clicks |

Rules for this account:
- **Proof and stories needs real material.** Nothing is published on the site yet (no reviews, no savings totals). Until the owner supplies a real, approved deal, fill this slot with the site's labelled "Example deal" numbers or the owner's own story, never an invented client.
- The launch weeks run heavier on Heroes and Offer on purpose. From week 3, hold Offer at 1 post a week and keep Heroes at 1.
- Cadence: 4-5 feed posts a week plus daily stories (2-4 frames: a poll or question sticker, a reshare of the latest post, a behind-the-scenes frame). Reels can run more often than carousels.
- Post times: weekdays 11 AM-1 PM or 7-9 PM Eastern, Sunday evening. Replace these with the account's own Insights once there is 30 days of data.
- Every week: at least 3 save posts (education, recap, checklists) and 2 send posts (Heroes, myth-busters).
