## 1. Voice fingerprint

- Sentence rhythm: short declaratives, then one longer sentence that carries the detail. "We negotiate. You just sign." / "One flat fee, one car."
- Signature openers: the number or the outcome first ("$995 → $0.", "One car. One flat $995.", "Got a dealer quote?"). Questions only when they are the buyer's real question.
- Punctuation habits: periods and commas. Colons for lists. **No em or en dashes, ever.** No exclamation marks in a row; rarely one at all.
- Words and phrases we use: flat fee, out-the-door, written recap, price, fees and trade-in, checked against market data, free call, nothing moves without your OK, from your couch to your driveway, we work for you, the dealer never pays us, heroes, those who serve.
- Words and phrases we NEVER use: fair, financing, loan, rate, APR, credit, lender, monthly payment, F&I, game-changer, revolutionary, unlock, elevate, seamless, hassle-free, best deal guaranteed, save thousands (unless a real, sourced number), "link in bio" as filler, "tag 3 friends", "comment YES".
- Emoji: none by default; at most 2 in a caption, never in a headline.
- Formatting (Instagram): hook in the first line, under 125 characters, readable on its own. Short lines with white space. Hashtags in the first comment. Reels: on-screen text carries the hook in frame 1. Carousels: 4:5, slide 1 is the promise, last slide is the summary plus one ask.

## 2. Who we are and who we write for

- We are: MyClearDeal, remote car buying advocates. We negotiate the vehicle price, dealer fees and trade-in for a flat fee and never get paid by the dealer.
- Audience (ICP):
  1. Busy car shoppers who dread the dealership, have a car in mind or a quote in hand, new or used, buy or lease.
  2. Heroes: firefighters, law enforcement, the medical field, teachers and military, active or retired, plus the people who love them (spouses, adult kids) who will send them the post.
  3. Spanish-speaking buyers (the site has a full ES version).
- Content pillars (see `../skills/ig-content-planner/references/mycleardeal-pillars.md`): Buyer education (40%), Proof and stories (25%), Heroes (20%), Offer (15%).

## 3. Hard rules (always / never)

- Always: name the specific thing (price, fees, trade-in, the written recap). Use real site facts. Label example numbers "Example deal". One idea per post. End on one ask.
- Always: spell MyClearDeal with capital M, C, D.
- Never: dashes, "fair", finance terms, hype words, invented numbers or reviews, dealership or car-brand logos, stock photos of people, engagement bait.
- Never promise a dollar savings amount. The site says: "Every deal is different, so we never promise a dollar amount."

## 4. Links and CTA

- Primary link: mycleardeal.com/start (Hero posts: mycleardeal.com/start?plan=hero; quote posts: mycleardeal.com/#review)
- Where it goes: the start form, which books a free consultation call (no payment taken on the site)
- CTA style: one ask per post, with the reason. Save posts: "Save this for your next car." Send posts: "Send this to a hero who is car shopping." Offer posts: "Book your free call" / "Claim my Hero deal" / "Review my quote". Never three asks at once.
