Our scope

What we do, and what we don't.

Three things, done well, checked against real market data.

We negotiate

Price, fees and trade-in.

  • The vehicle price
  • Dealer fees and add-ons
  • Your trade-in value

We don't handle

Financing and optional products

Financing, interest rates, or optional products like extended warranties or GAP. Those are between you and the dealer. They are always optional, and you can say no to any of them.
