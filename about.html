The basics

What exactly does myClearDeal do?

We negotiate the vehicle price, dealer fees and add-ons, and your trade-in, and we check the price against real market data. You get a written out-the-door recap before you agree to anything.

Do I ever have to go to the dealership?

Only once, to sign and pick up your car. All the shopping and negotiating happens remotely with us by phone, text and email.

Do you help with leases and used cars?

Yes. New, used and leases. On a lease we negotiate the vehicle price, dealer fees and trade-in the same way we do on a purchase.

Fees and trust

How much does it cost?

One flat fee of $995 for our help with one car purchase. It is the same for everyone, with no tiers, add-ons or percentage of your savings. We confirm it with you on your free consultation call, before any work starts. See what is included.

Is there anything else I pay you?

No. Taxes, registration and the price of the car are paid to the dealer as usual, and we make sure each of those numbers is right. Our fee is the $995 and nothing more.

Are you working for me or for the dealer?

For you. You pay us, the dealer does not. We aim to be fair to you and fair to the dealer: a market price, not a fight. That keeps your deal moving without games.

Is the free quote review really free?

Yes. Send us the dealer worksheet and a member of our team will review it personally and follow up with what looks fair and what to question. It is a hands-on review by a person, not an automated tool. No obligation to hire us.

Pricing and refunds

What does the fee cover?

Our help with one vehicle purchase: the free consultation, vehicle sourcing, negotiating the vehicle price, dealer fees and add-ons, and your trade-in, a market-data check on the price, and a written out-the-door recap before you agree. See pricing.

Does starting a deal charge me?

No. The form only books your free consultation. No payment is ever taken on this website. We confirm the fee with you on that call, before any work begins.

Is my trade-in included?

Yes. Valuing your trade and keeping it separate from the new deal is included. No extra charge.

Is it worth it for my deal?

Every deal is different, so we never promise a dollar amount. On your free call we will tell you honestly whether we think we can help. If your deal already looks clean, we will say so.

What is your refund policy?

Our fee and any refund terms are set out in your written client service agreement, which you review and sign before any work begins and before any payment is made. Unless that agreement says otherwise, fees for work already performed are not refundable. If this summary differs from your signed agreement, the agreement controls.

What we don't cover

Do you help with financing or warranties?

No. We negotiate the vehicle price, fees and trade-in. Financing and optional products are between you and the dealer, and they're always optional.
