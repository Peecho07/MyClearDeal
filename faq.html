The basics

What exactly does myClearDeal do?+

We negotiate your whole car deal for you: the vehicle price, dealer fees and add-ons, your trade-in, your financing rate and any protection products. You get a written recap of every number before you agree to anything.

Do I ever have to go to the dealership?+

Only to sign and pick up your car, unless the dealer delivers. All the shopping, negotiating and paperwork review happens remotely with us by phone, text and email.

Do you help with leases and used cars?+

Yes. New, used and leases, including the money factor, residual and fees that make lease math confusing.

The finance office

Will you tell me to skip every warranty and add-on?+

No. We tell you which protection products make sense for how you drive and what they should cost, so you buy only what is worth it.

Can you help if I already have financing?+

Yes. We will compare your pre-approval with what the dealer offers and make sure the rate and term on the contract match what you agreed to.
