# MyClearDeal launch set: captions and posting plan

Written with the MyClearDeal-adapted Instagram skills in `.claude/skills/` (caption writer, carousel planner,
hashtag strategist, content planner, humanizer audit). Facts match `.claude/references/mycleardeal-brand.md`
and the live site as of Nov 25, 2026.

How each entry is built:
- **Line 1 is the hook.** Under 125 characters and readable before Instagram's "more" fold.
- **One ask per post**, with the reason (save it, send it, or book).
- **Hashtags go in the first comment:** 5 per post, sized 2-3 niche, 1-2 mid and 1 broad (see `.claude/skills/ig-hashtag-strategist/references/mycleardeal-hashtag-bank.md`). Check each tag's post count in Instagram search the first time you use it.
- **Reels are silent.** Add a track in the app. The words are burned in.

Files: `carousels/<name>/slide-NN.png` (1080x1350), `reels/<name>.mp4` + `-cover.png` (1080x1920). Review it all in `contact-sheet.html`.

**Pin to the profile:** Hero announcement reel, Hero launch carousel, "We negotiate. You just sign." carousel.

**Daily Stories (2 to 4 frames):**
- Reshare the latest feed post. Instagram treats this like a send.
- One poll or question sticker:
  - Poll: "Did your last dealer quote have add-ons you didn't ask for? Yes / No"
  - Quiz: "What's included in out-the-door? Price only / Price + fees + taxes"
  - Question box: "What's on your dealer quote that confuses you?"
- On Hero weeks: "Know a hero who's car shopping? Send them this."

**First hour after each post:** reply to every comment. Early replies tell Instagram the post is worth showing to more people.
